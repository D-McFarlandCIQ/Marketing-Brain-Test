### The problem we solve

Revenue teams carry a bigger number every year. Hitting it means making better
decisions faster when the market changes, and most companies leave that to chance
because planning, comp, and results live in separate systems and spreadsheets.
Existing tools each force a tradeoff:

- **Spreadsheets** are flexible until you grow.
- **No-code tools** are fast until your plans outgrow them.
- **Legacy suites** scale, but they run on consultants and overnight batch jobs.
  "Bring a legacy suite a new pricing model and they'll tell you to run a simpler comp plan."

### Why us: the live calculation layer

> Every alternative claims AI, enterprise scale, and governance, but none of it is
> possible without a live calculation layer underneath, and that's the one thing they
> don't have. We do. We call it SmartGrid.

SmartGrid is "our unified modeling workspace where you build and manage your plans,
quotas, territories, and payouts." A change in one area updates everything else the
moment it happens. See [product/capabilities/smartgrid.md](../product/capabilities/smartgrid.md).

### Three pillars (Growth leads; the other two support it)

| Pillar | Lead persona | What it means (verbatim) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Drive revenue growth** | CRO / sales leader | Reps see what their deals pay and chase the pipeline that moves your number. When strategy changes, plans, quotas, and territories catch up in days, so you capture growth the moment it appears. |
| **Trust every number** | CFO / finance / CISO | Payouts, accruals, and agent actions trace back to their sources and hold up with reps, auditors, and the board. AI works from the same governed numbers as everyone else, so its outputs are provable and compliant. |
| **Scale without adding headcount** | Comp practitioners / CFO | The analysts who run comp can build and change any plan themselves, while agents absorb the routine load. The same team can run a bigger, more complex program, with no consultants or new hires. |

**Drive revenue growth: capability proof**
- Build any plan your strategy calls for, splits, accelerators, and exceptions included, and change it in days
- Give reps live statements with deal pay, accelerator progress, and a what-if calculator
- Design territories and quotas on live CRM data, with AI recommending coverage
- Test the revenue and spend impact of any plan or headcount change before you commit

**Trust every number: capability proof**
- Trace payouts and calculations to their sources, with the audit trail built in
- SOX-compliant controls and enterprise-grade governance
- Reps get a live view of their pay and the calculation behind it
- Agents work from one live, governed dataset and leave the same audit trail as a person

**Scale without adding headcount: capability proof**
- Build and change plans in an Excel-like interface with no proprietary syntax, or in plain language with an agent
- Agents draft answers to rep pay questions and handle payout prep and QA
- Automated workflows run payouts, plan administration, and plan agreements
- Planning and comp run on one data structure, so every product works from the same governed numbers

### Growth flywheel: Plan → Pay → Monitor → Adapt

| Stage | Headline | Key points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan | Set quotas and territories that match the market | Model territories, quotas, capacity on live data; build plans in plain language with the Comp Builder and Rev Planning agents; forecast with predictive models |
| Pay | Pay every rep accurately and on time | No-code logic for commissions, bonuses, splits; trace payouts to source; Comp Ops agent handles routine pay questions; reconciled close with accruals finance trusts |
| Monitor | Act while it still counts | Track attainment, payout, spend against plan; flag payout anomalies before payroll; ask plain-language questions; connect your own AI tools through the MCP Server |
| Adapt | Grow faster with every cycle | Re-model quota, territory, or plan changes on live data; change in-flight plans yourself or through an agent; redeploy in days |

### Rules from the V6 brief

- **Lead with growth**, not agility, automation, efficiency, or cost reduction. The July 2026
  Gartner MQ flagged that our messaging "centers on cost reduction and efficiency rather
  than broader CSO priorities."
- **Make growth claims direct.** "Reps understand their pay, so they sell more" is indirect
  and undifferentiated. Direct levers: live comp reps act on, predictive signals,
  territory and quota configuration.
- **Tell one platform story.** Don't pitch ICM, Sales Planning, and Catalyst as separate
  products. The story has to absorb EPM and future agents without a relaunch.
- **AI is native, configurable, and opt-in**, never "bolted on".
- **Every claim must be provable.** "Anything without proof waits." See
  [proof-points.md](proof-points.md).

### Open items in V6

- Exec feedback asks for a Slides pitch format instead of a framework doc, with no framework jargon.
- The **SmartGrid vocabulary** decision is still open. Feedback warns that SmartGrid is
  becoming a blanket word and that "data" is the wrong center. A simpler candidate line:
  "SmartGrid is the unified modeling workspace that allows you to maximize growth as a system."
- The three candidate "growth is left to chance" problem lines in the doc were drafted
  with AI and are **not approved**. Don't use them.

## Previous framework: FY27 platform "planning and incentives in sync"

Last complete framework (owner in Drive: Lindsey Bly). Its proof section is marked
**[TO BE UPDATED]**. Still the source for the current first-call deck.

- **Audience:** incentive comp and sales planning owners (finance, RevOps, comp
  leaders), Director to VP, mid-market to lower-end enterprise. Industries: tech,
  financial services, business services, manufacturing. B2B with high ACV.
- **Problem:** market uncertainty ends "set it and forget it" annual planning. Teams
  constantly re-plan, and the tools can't keep up. Coined term: **"Planning Whiplash"**.
- **Competing alternatives:** "Legacy SPM platforms and Excel spreadsheets that aren't
  designed for speed, flexibility, or alignment."
- **Value promise:** "Planning and incentives in sync—finally. CaptivateIQ bridges
  incentive compensation management and sales planning to bring quota, territory,
  headcount, and compensation together in a single AI-infused workspace built for
  agility, alignment, and scale."
- **Pillars:** Connected workspace ("One source of truth from capacity planning to
  payouts"), Unbeatable flexibility ("Plan for anything, pivot anytime"), Intelligent
  optimization ("Make better decisions, faster").
- **Business impact:** revenue resilience through operational efficiency, revenue growth,
  and predictability.
