## What we already know (from research, not from the personas deck)

The 2025 State of Incentive Compensation tracker describes the people who run comp
programs. Most respondents were Senior Manager to EVP (71%). Most decide with input
from others (38%) or as part of a committee (40%). Two-thirds (66%) are directly
involved in plan design. Their top business objectives are supporting growth
initiatives (59%), seller productivity (56%), and seller motivation (53%). Their top
challenge is a lack of visibility into comp program ROI. See
[library/research/state-of-incentive-compensation-2025.md](../../library/research/state-of-incentive-compensation-2025.md).
