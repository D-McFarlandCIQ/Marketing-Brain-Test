## New business

### "CaptivateIQ is a glorified Excel / Excel in the cloud."

The landmine every competitor sets (Xactly, Varicent, Spiff, QuotaPath, Performio).
QuotaPath's variant: "If you have problems in Excel and put it in software that's also
like Excel, those problems won't go away." The written response isn't in Notion; it was
a video.

**Working response (draft, from V6 and win/loss evidence):**
- Lead with the point: Excel-like is the interface, not the engine. SmartGrid is a live,
  governed calculation layer with audit trails, workflows, SOX-compliant controls, and
  enterprise scale. A spreadsheet has none of that.
- The familiarity is a reason customers buy. Won deals cite it directly: a new admin
  could own the system (Hawthorne CAT, 2024), and "ability to change things as easily as
  Excel is a huge gamechanger" (Sojern, 2024).
- No proprietary syntax means changes don't wait on services. Contrast Spiff's
  SQL-like language and Performio's SQL transforms.

### "We'll keep running it in spreadsheets."

See [competitors/spreadsheets.md](competitors/spreadsheets.md).

### "We need enterprise scale and proof of maturity."

Lost-deal evidence (CBRE, 2025) shows enterprise buyers shortlist from Gartner and
Forrester and weigh F500 logos and user scale. Lead with analyst recognition (subject to
the usage rules in [proof-points.md](proof-points.md)), SOX-compliant controls, audit
trail, and governance. Ask PMM for current enterprise references.

## Cross-sell objections (Incentives customers to Planning)

Pattern: **Agree → Probe (trap question) → Pivot**, always ending in a RevOps intro or
cc. Source: [cross-sell-icm-to-planning.md](cross-sell-icm-to-planning.md).

| Objection | Agree | Probe | Pivot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "We already started our planning." | It rarely makes sense to switch midway. | "Do you usually only re-plan once a year, or do you adjust as quotas shift?" | Most teams are surprised we can implement well before their next re-plan. Ask for the RevOps intro for next cycle. |
| "This isn't in the budget." | Adding tools to the budget is hard. | "When budget does open up, what problems usually rise to the top?" or "Is there zero room, or do new tools need to pay for themselves quickly?" | Planning cuts hours of rework every quarter, so it often moves up the list. People with a directional sense of what they want get the budget when it frees up. |
| "Another team handles that, separate from comp." | That's common. | "When that team finalizes quotas, do you get visibility right away or do you wait?" | With Planning, admins see quota progress and the final numbers feed commissions directly, so they start earlier and stop cleaning up. |
| "Send me something and I'll pass it along." | Happy to. | "What would be most useful for your team to see?" | Many RevOps teams can tell within 15 minutes whether Planning would help. Offer a short call. |
