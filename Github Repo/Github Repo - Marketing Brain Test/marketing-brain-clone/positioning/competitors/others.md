# Other competitors

Internal only. Promote any of these to its own file once there's a battlecard.

## Oracle

Forrester Leader (Q1 2025). Very flexible and suited to large Oracle-ecosystem
enterprises, but it needs technical skills, which slows time to value. Forrester gave it
top marks on disputes, reporting, workflow, controls, and crediting, and low marks on time
to value and ease of implementation. Lead with time to value and admin self-service.

## Anaplan

Forrester Strong Performer. Flexible and tied into planning, but few out-of-the-box
features and a UI that needs work. Relevant for **Planning** and **EPM** deals. An
Anaplan battlecard is referenced in Aligned (not read). 19% aided awareness.

## Pigment

Planning and EPM competitor. Aligned battlecard dated April 2026, internal only (not
read). PMM has an open task for an EPM displacement playbook vs Pigment and Varicent.

## Salesforce (native planning)

Comes up in **Planning** deals. Gong greatest hits: Adaptive Bio (how we win and lose vs
Salesforce) and Red Nucleus (why we win vs Salesforce). No written card.

## Forma.ai

Forrester Strong Performer and **Customer Favorite**. Software plus services, fast time
to value, limited self-configuration. Contrast: our admins configure plans themselves.

## beqom

Forrester Contender: a total-compensation platform with thin SPM/ICM features. Probably
the winner in our CBRE loss (2025, enterprise, $1B bonus program). The buyer chose a
vendor focused on bonuses and comp planning, with more F500 logos and 70K+ user scale.
Lesson: show flexibility beyond commissions (bonuses) and enterprise scale proof.

## NICE

Forrester Contender. Flexible crediting engine and strong end-user modeling; weak UI and
slow time to value.

## Concert Finance

Acquired by Varicent. Legacy 2021 card only.
