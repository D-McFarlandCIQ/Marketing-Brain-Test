## Six reasons enterprises choose us (compete slides, Aug 2026)

1. Quick, easy plan changes: no-code, formula syntax
2. Lower TCO: straightforward, predictable pricing
3. Faster time to value: no-code data layer and modeling engine
4. White-glove support from a dedicated team of comp experts
5. Try before you buy: a proof of concept in days
6. Innovation leader, next-gen platform

## Their weaknesses (2024 one-pager, stale; qualitative)

High software and implementation costs; a partner-first model with frequent change
orders; extra fees for admin users, data storage, and transactions; a rigid table
structure that needs consultants or paid service hours; a platform built by
acquisition; long, hard proofs of concept.

## Where they're strong

Brand recognition, enterprise scale and customization, a full SPM suite, sandbox
(2023 card). Forrester agrees on breadth and AI depth.

## Pricing intel

Structure only, undated and unsourced (from a 2021–23 card): admin and payee licences
per user per month, an annual platform fee, and a one-time implementation fee, packaged
as Standard, Professional, and Enterprise. No price points.
