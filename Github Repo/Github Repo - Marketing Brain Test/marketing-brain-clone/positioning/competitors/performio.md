## Six reasons (compete slides, Aug 2026)

customer proof and references · commitment to innovation · any plan, any complexity.

## Where we win (2022 card, stale)

Excel-like self-serve vs Performio's SQL for data transforms (their demos pre-build
tables to hide it), so customers depend on services for changes. Disputes are less
auditable than our inquiries and overrides. Ignore the old funding and headcount
comparisons in the card; they're years out of date.

## Where they're strong

Forrester credits workflow and dispute resolution, which are areas where we scored 3/5.
Don't pick a fight there.
