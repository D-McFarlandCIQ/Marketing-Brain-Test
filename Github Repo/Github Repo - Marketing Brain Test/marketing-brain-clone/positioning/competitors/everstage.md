# Everstage

Internal only.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Who | No-code ICM, founded 2020. Mid-market and small enterprise. |
| Forrester Q1 2025 | Strong Performer. Modern no-code spreadsheet UI and a seller-modeling module (Crystal). Dispute, workflow, and document management are less mature. Top marks on adoption and pricing. |
| Current battlecard | [Aligned](https://my.teamaligned.com/library/pMMzkfmBt/battlecard-everstage-2025) |

## Positioning (buying guide, Feb 2026: "When payroll is on the line")

"Everstage may look easy at first, but teams often run into issues when plans change,
questions come up, or payouts need to be explained."

- **Data syncing:** we have an integrated submission form (they rely on external
  uploads), sync failure logs (they troubleshoot "by trial and error"), a transformation
  log, pre-import validation, duplicate handling, and re-syncs of only changed rows.
- **Plan structure:** mostly parity. We claim more consistent history and deeper drill-through.
- **Day-to-day:** they need vendor help for rep-specific rules and overrides, and
  workflow assignment is limited.

## Six reasons (compete slides, Aug 2026)

Business agility (no-code, spreadsheet-like) · any plan, any complexity · pre-built
reports and dashboards · admins and payees see calculations in real time · permissions
and workflows for large orgs · dedicated US-based support and implementation.

## Where they're strong

Price. Expect aggressive discounting.

## Proof

OpenGov (2024, won, strategic): picked us over Everstage despite its lower price.
Reasons: real-time results, fewer clicks, US-based support, dashboards, and a
transparent sales process. Their view of Everstage: offshore support with midnight
replies, a slow UI (minutes of processing), and plan management "too basic".
