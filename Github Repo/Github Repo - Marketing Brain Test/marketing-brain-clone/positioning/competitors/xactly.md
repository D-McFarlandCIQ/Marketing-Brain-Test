## How to position (take-out play, Aug 2026)

"Our objective isn't to convince customers that Xactly is a poor solution." Target teams
that have **outgrown a traditional ICM approach**, and pitch CaptivateIQ as the step from
ICM to SPM, not a feature comparison. Themes enterprise comp leaders raise: plans change
more often; they must move faster with governance; spreadsheets still fill gaps for
modeling, reporting, and exceptions; leadership wants strategic insight; teams are trying AI.

ENT opener: "Calculating commissions isn't the challenge anymore. Keeping up with the business is."

## Six reasons (compete slides, Aug 2026)

1. Faster time to value: out-of-the-box integrations and flexible data layer
2. Lower TCO: no-code, formula syntax
3. Flexibility and agility: SmartGrid real-time modeling
4. Incentive-led growth: an analytics suite you can actually use
5. Seller experience
6. Modern technology

## Their weaknesses (2024 one-pager and 2022 card, stale)

Rigid import templates and black-box ETL; plan changes go through professional
services ("playing telephone with services"); batch processing for mid-period audits.
The 2022 card claims Xactly charges about 2x for implementation, which is unsourced.

## Where they're strong

Forecasting and territory planning (2022). Now that we sell Planning, re-check this.

## Their landmine

"CaptivateIQ is a glorified Excel." See [../objections.md](../objections.md).
