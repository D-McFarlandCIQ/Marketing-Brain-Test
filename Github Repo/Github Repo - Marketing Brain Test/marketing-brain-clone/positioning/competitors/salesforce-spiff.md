Internal only.

## Six reasons (compete slides, Aug 2026)

1. Full ownership of commissions
2. Easy expansion: open canvas
3. End-to-end commissions with quick reporting
4. Commitment to innovation
5. Comp-focused service
6. High customer satisfaction

## Where we win (2022 card, stale; check against the current card)

- Their rule and template approach can't handle edge-case plan components.
- Admins must learn a proprietary SQL-like language. We use Excel-like formulas.
- Basic integrations with no import modes, so deleted Salesforce records persist. We
  offer multiple import modes per connector.
- Their ASC 606 tool is limited and expensive, so get the prospect on a 606 review call.

## Where they're strong (2022)

Polished UI and rep mobile app, rep commission forecasting, easier for non-Excel users,
NetSuite integration, well-regarded support.

## Their landmines

"CIQ has to sell Premier Support because it can't support your data" (no written
response exists) and "glorified Excel".

## Proof

- Sojern (2024, won, 367 payees): Spiff was the main competitor. After a CaptivateIQ vs
  Spiff comparison the buyer "didn't even look at" Spiff.
- Forter left Spiff (couldn't handle SDR plans or self-manage ASC 606). Lattice moved
  Spiff → Xactly → CaptivateIQ ("flexible and easy to make changes in").
