# Spreadsheets

Internal only. The most common alternative: 47% of companies in our 2025 research use
spreadsheets for incentive comp, and 14% use nothing else.

## Incentives vs spreadsheets

**Where we win**
1. **One connected workspace** for capacity, territory, quota, and incentives. Changes roll
   through the whole process instead of being rebuilt in Excel; no version sprawl.
2. **Automation** from data integration through calculation to payroll. Calculations that
   take hours run in seconds.
3. **Transparency.** Reps see commissions in real time, which cuts inquiries.
4. **Accuracy.** Fewer errors and disputes. Don't say "always accurate"; the battlecard's
   absolute claim isn't provable.
5. **Scale.** Add payees and adjustments without rebuilding models.
6. **Flexibility.** Change plans in real time without IT.

**Why they stay on spreadsheets, and our responses**

| Reason | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "It's free." | The hidden cost of manual errors, wasted time, and overpayments adds up. Our research: 48% of companies both overpaid and underpaid sellers last year, and payout processing takes a median 36 hours a month. |
| Comfort and familiarity | CaptivateIQ is spreadsheet-like and easy to learn: the familiarity of spreadsheets with the power of automation. |
| "We're not complex enough yet." | It saves time even for small teams, and starting early makes scaling easier. |
| Implementation fear | Our team handles data migration with minimal disruption and no outside consultants. |
| Control | Same control as spreadsheets, plus real-time visibility and reporting. |

## Planning vs spreadsheets

**Where we win:** real-time scenario modeling with side-by-side comparison (planning
cycles shrink from weeks to hours); audit trail, version history, and approvals;
territory and quota outputs flow into commissions with no exports; transparent
assignments; adding reps or territories takes minutes.

**Extra objection: "Planning only happens once a year."** Response: with today's
macro environment, planning isn't annual anymore, and poor plans ripple into quota
disputes, missed coverage, and comp adjustments that cost far more than the tool.
Some teams also fear change, so stress that Planning can replicate their exact process.

## Proof

- Unnamed Planning customer: 80,000+ accounts; 75% less time on planning (one month to
  one week); "doesn't crash my laptop." Internal only until PMM attributes it.
- Unnamed Incentives customer: 60+ hours a month on 12 plans and 60+ statements; annual
  plan creation went from 60 hours to five.
- Win/loss: Hawthorne CAT (2024, 10+ years of manual spreadsheets) chose us for control
  and an Excel-like language a new admin could own. Ben E. Keith Foods ran 2,000 payees on spreadsheets.
- Loss: CBRE (2025, Excel-based $1B bonus program) went to a bonus-focused vendor. See [others.md](others.md#beqom).
