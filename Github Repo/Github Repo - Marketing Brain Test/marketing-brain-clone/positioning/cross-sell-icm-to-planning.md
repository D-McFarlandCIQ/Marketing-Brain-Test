# Cross-sell play: Incentives (ICM) to Planning

For CSMs talking to an existing **Incentives** customer. The goal is to **earn an intro
to RevOps, not to pitch a product.**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Audience | Current CaptivateIQ customers |
| Persona | Incentive comp admin (a stakeholder, not the buyer) |
| Ask | "Become a Planning Champion and introduce us to RevOps" |
| Buyer | RevOps (owns quota and territory) |

## The pain (the admin's view)

- Admins wait months for quotas to be finalized before they can build comp plans.
- Quotas change quarter over quarter, so admins rebuild plans all year.
- Poor communication between Sales Ops and comp leads to last-minute scrambles to hit deadlines.

## Pitches (verbatim)

**Short (<35 words):** "CaptivateIQ Planning links quota modeling with incentive comp.
Instead of waiting for an Excel File, admins can set up plans faster, make changes
without a ton of rework, and stay aligned with Sales Ops."

**Cheat-sheet version:** "Planning and Incentives are connected processes. When quotas
change, it creates extra work for you—rebuilding plans, adjusting payouts, and racing
to hit deadlines. CaptivateIQ Planning links quota modeling directly with Incentive
Comp, so comp plans stay accurate automatically. Less waiting, less rework, and fewer
late scrambles."

## Value props

1. **Launch the moment quotas are set**, instead of chasing spreadsheets or waiting
   months for RevOps. Quota changes flow into compensation without manual uploads, and
   Salesforce sync keeps plans and quotas matched.
2. **Eliminate the late-night scramble.** Real-time quota updates flow straight into
   incentives and cut plan changes from days to minutes.
3. **Stay aligned and on schedule** with one shared workspace for quotas and comp. See
   how quota scenarios affect downstream comp plans before deadlines hit.

## Before the call

- Recent comp plan changes or delays
- Signs of quota shifts: new roles, reorgs, hiring, attrition
- Support tickets about comp errors or missed deadlines

## Triggers to listen for

- "We're struggling to get comp plans out on time."
- "RevOps adjusts quotas every quarter."
- "We're constantly rebuilding plans."
- "We're merging teams or handling a lot of turnover." (Also M&A, promotions, role moves.)

## Discovery questions

- How long after quotas are set before plans go live?
- What happens when quotas change mid-year?
- Do you usually get enough lead time from RevOps?
- How do late changes affect your deadlines?

QBR opener example: "I noticed your comp plans have been adjusted the past two quarters.
Has the business been updating quotas to adjust for the market?"

## Call to action (verbatim)

"I know quota changes can create extra work for you in commissions, or even delay your
comp plans. Our Planning module connects directly to incentives, so when RevOps adjusts
quotas, your plans stay accurate without all the rework. Would you be open to
introducing me to your RevOps co-part so we can explore how to make that connection for
your team?"

## Objections

Handled in [objections.md](objections.md#cross-sell-objections-incentives-customers-to-planning).

## Tone

Consultative, not salesy. Anchor on their workload and deadlines. Ask curious, open questions.
