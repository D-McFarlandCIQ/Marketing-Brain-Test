## Market segments from our research

The 2025 State of Incentive Compensation tracker (n=205, U.S., B2B, 500+ employees)
found four buyer segments. Use these as a lens, not as an ICP.

| Segment | Share | Profile | Implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High-performing ICM users | 31% | ICM software plus spreadsheets; mostly 500–5,000 employees; median 11–20 plans; most advanced AI use | Most sophisticated buyers; likely competitive replacement deals |
| Mainstream ICM users | 39% | ICM software only; mostly 500–5,000 employees; median 6–10 plans | Replacement deals |
| Spreadsheet users | 17% | Mostly spreadsheets; mostly 1,000–10,000 employees; median 1–5 plans | Greenfield; see [competitors/spreadsheets.md](competitors/spreadsheets.md) |
| Custom-solution users | 13% | Mostly home-grown builds; 43% at 5,000+ employees; median 1–5 plans | Build-vs-buy conversation |

## Deal-stage signals from the win/loss process

The win/loss process treats deals of **$150K ARR or more** as large enough to bring an
exec sponsor into the interview. It's a working threshold, not a segment definition.
