# Logo, marks, and lockups

CIQ has three marks, ranked from primary to smallest. **Always get the files from the
brand team or the Notion Brand Portal** ([resources.md](../resources.md)). Never
redraw a mark, take one from a screenshot, or recolor one.

| Mark | What it is | When to use | Min width | Clear space |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **CIQ Wordmark** | "CaptivateIQ" in a custom geometric sans | The primary identifier, and the default for everything | **116 px** | At least the width of the "Q", on all sides |
| **CIQ Shorthand Mark** | "CIQ" | A secondary mark or sign-off, alongside a full Wordmark that is the hero. Also fine in internal and social use. Managed by request through the brand team. | **42 px** | Similar to the Wordmark |
| **IQ Bug** | "IQ" | Very limited, space-constrained spots: favicons, the in-product drawer | **26 px** | Similar to the Wordmark |

The Shorthand Mark and the IQ Bug **sit near the Wordmark but are never locked up
with it.** Ask the brand team whether to use the Bug or the Shorthand for a given
placement.

## Wordmark details (p. 30)

- The angles of the "v" and the tail of the "Q" both sit at **20°**. That angle is
  the root of the whole graphic language (see
  [graphic-language.md](graphic-language.md)).
- The "I" has a cap and base, a monospace nod to code, so "Captivate" and "IQ" read
  as two words even in one color.

## Wordmark color (pp. 31–32)

Use **dark navy (Galaxy) or white** most of the time. Use black or white only for
black-and-white print.

**Recommended (high contrast):**

| Background | Wordmark |
| --- | --- |
| Galaxy `#0A2028` | White `#FFFFFF` |
| Taupe 2 `#DBD7D3` | Galaxy `#0A2028` |
| Taupe 1 `#F5F4F3` | Galaxy `#0A2028` |
| Lagoon 4 `#114753` | Hedge green `#B6F071` |
| Green `#A7D841` | Galaxy `#0A2028` |
| Light blue `#E8F3FC` | Galaxy `#0A2028` |

**More expressive:**

| Background | Wordmark |
| --- | --- |
| Legacy teal `#0F303C` | Warm white `#E5E3E1` |
| Light green `#C0FFA1` | Legacy teal `#14404F` |
| Violet (see open question 2) | Cirrus 1 `#F1F8FC` |

The recommended backgrounds are guidance, not a hard rule. The Wordmark sometimes
sits on other secondary or accent colors.

## Sizing in a layout (p. 35)

Suggested Wordmark width as a share of the format width: **square 35–40%,
horizontal 20–25%, portrait 10–15%.** For the Shorthand and the IQ Bug, the manual
gives 20–25% for square and horizontal formats and 10–15% for portrait. Check the
placement with the brand design team.

- **Do** put a dark mark on light or mid-tone backgrounds, and a white mark on dark
  or mid-tone ones.
- **Do** put a white mark on high-contrast graphics or photos, with brand team
  approval.

## Restrictions (all three marks, pp. 34, 41, 46)

Don't:

- split the mark or move letters onto different planes
- flip or rotate it
- pair clashing colors
- change or add colors
- resize parts of it or change its silhouette
- make it 3D
- crop it or let it run off the composition
- squash or stretch it
- add a tagline or other content to it, unless the brand team created that version

## Partner co-branding (pp. 36–37)

- Lock the CIQ Wordmark up with the partner logo using the manual's spacing. **The CIQ
  Wordmark is slightly larger**, because it's primary.
- Don't offset or split the two logos. Don't flip or rotate the lockup. Never make
  CIQ smaller than the partner. Don't stack or redesign the lockup unless the brand
  team did it.
- **The brand team reviews every partner lockup.**

## Product lockups (pp. 61–88)

Each product pillar has a lockup: the CIQ Wordmark (or Shorthand Mark) followed by the
product name in **Manrope Light**.

| Product | Lockups |
| --- | --- |
| CIQ Incentives | Wordmark + "Incentives"; Shorthand + "Incentives" |
| CIQ Planning | Wordmark + "Planning"; Shorthand + "Planning" |
| CIQ Catalyst | Wordmark + "Catalyst"; Shorthand + "Catalyst" |
| CIQ Agents | Wordmark + "Agents"; Shorthand + "Agents" |

- **The horizontal lockup is the default.** Use the stacked version only in very
  limited cases.
- Product lockups and their palettes **aren't self-service.** Use them only in
  approved designs and templates, or get approval first. For colors, see
  [product-palettes.md](product-palettes.md).

## Open questions for Brand

1. **Off-palette wordmark colors.** Several wordmark pairings use values close to, but
   not in, the palette: `#B6F071` (Hedge 2 is `#B5EF71`), `#A7D841` (legacy Hedge is
   `#A6D741`), `#E8F3FC`, `#C0FFA1`, and `#E5E3E1`. Are these intentional and
   separate, or should they snap to palette values?
2. **Mislabeled swatch.** The third "expressive" pairing on p. 32 is visibly violet,
   but its label reads `#0A2028` (Galaxy). What's the background, Violet 3 `#7F3BFF`?
3. **Lockup typeface.** Product lockups use Manrope Light, but the typography section
   says Manrope is being phased out (see [typography.md](typography.md)). Will the
   lockups keep Manrope?
