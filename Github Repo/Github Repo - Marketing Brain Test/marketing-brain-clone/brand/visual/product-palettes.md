# Product pillar palettes

Each product pillar has its own color family and pattern, so offerings can be told
apart within the platform. **The CIQ brand and design team maintains this system. It
isn't self-service.** Use it inside approved designs and templates, or get approval
first. Lockups are covered in [logo.md](logo.md#product-lockups-pp-6188).

Every pillar palette is **anchored by Galaxy and Taupe**, so they read as one family.
The palettes are meant to flex. You can add or adjust colors as long as the tonal
spectrum stays similar.

Type in every pillar: **White or Taupe 1** `#F5F4F3`, **Lagoon 4** `#114753`, or
**Almost Black** `#0C1728`.

## At a glance

| Pillar | Hue | Gradient spectrum | Dark anchor | Pattern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Incentives** | Green → blue | Hedge 1 `#E0FFB3` → Hedge 2 `#B5EF71` → Cirrus 4 `#339EB8` | Hedge Dark `#074F37` | Concentric curved bands, chartreuse through emerald to a deep teal edge. Flexibility, expansion, growth. |
| **Planning** | Violet → pink | Violet 1 `#EBE1FF` → Berry 3.5 `#DD88CD` → Violet 3 `#7F3BFF` | Violet Dark `#250547` | A smooth diagonal gradient through violet and indigo, with soft bands of light and shadow. Depth, structure, foresight. |
| **Catalyst** | Earth tones ("Apricot") | Apricot 1 `#F4FFB7` → Apricot 2 `#FFD89C` → Apricot 3 `#F98D55` | Apricot Dark `#3F1A07` | Overlapping diagonal chevrons, deep brown through amber to cream. Momentum, driving change. |
| **Agents** | Full spectrum | Hedge 2 `#B5EF71` → Violet 3 `#7F3BFF` → Apricot 3 `#F98D55`, plus Cirrus 4 `#339EB8` and Hedge 1 `#E0FFB3` | Galaxy `#0A2028` | A sweeping radial arc across the whole spectrum. The Agents thread the three pillar colors together into one experience. |

**Apricot** is a special tweaked color set made only for Catalyst. It isn't part of
the main palette. **Berry 3.5** `#DD88CD` is a bridge color made for Planning.

The Agents palette also nods to **Cirrus**, "the shade that originally represented
CIQ."

## Open questions for Brand

1. **Agents pattern copy (p. 90)** maps green to Catalyst and orange to Incentives.
   The palettes have it the other way round: Incentives is green and Catalyst is
   orange (Apricot). Which is right? This file assumes the palettes are.
2. **Incentives pattern copy (p. 66)** opens "To represent CIQ Planning…". It's
   probably copied from the Planning page.
3. **Catalyst palette (p. 81)** is captioned "Planning gradient spectrum", another
   likely copy error.
4. **Berry 3.5 `#DD88CD`** doesn't match the full-spectrum Berry bridge `#E891DB`. Is
   it a deliberate Planning-only value?
5. **Catalyst example (p. 83)** has a base of `#3F1A07` with Hedge layers
   (`#E0FFB3 – #B5EF71`), and another example with a malformed base, `F9F9F8 7`. Are
   these errors?
