# Color

The palette is meant to convey **intelligence and wonder.** Purples, greens, and deep
teals provide the energy. The warmth of taupe and slate, light blue, and white keeps
it grounded. The brand uses **very few flat colors**. Colors blend, layer, and move.

Machine-readable values for every swatch below, plus the full spectrum, are in
[color-tokens.yaml](color-tokens.yaml).

## Simplified palette (p. 50): use this by default

Each family runs from **1 (lightest) to 4**, then a **Dark**. Use the 25% and 50% tints
of step 1 only as subtle background washes.

## Full spectrum (p. 49)

Behind the simplified palette is a **full spectrum** for smooth gradients. Between the
numbered steps of each family sit:

- **Bridge colors** ("use more sparingly"): intermediate steps for blends.
- **Legacy colors** ("use as needed"): carried over from the previous palette.

Several values that look off-palette in the layering examples are actually here:
`#9867FF` (legacy Violet), `#14404F` and `#0F303C` (legacy Lagoon), `#2D7B81`
(legacy Lagoon), `#F98D55` (legacy Citrus, also Apricot 3), and `#EFB6A6` (Berry
bridge). Every value is in [color-tokens.yaml](color-tokens.yaml).

## Proportion (p. 51)

1. **Generous white space** comes first.
2. **Lagoon, Violet, and Hedge** are the families used most in the system.
3. **Slate, Taupe, Cirrus**, and their tints are the most-used secondaries.
4. **Berry and Citrus** appear only in small, high-impact details.
5. Use **Dark** swatches very sparingly.

## Type color (pp. 52–53)

**Set type in Almost Black or White almost always.** Use Lagoon, Violet, and Hedge
sparingly for moments of emphasis. Never set long passages in an accent or Dark color.

## Building a composition (pp. 52–54)

- **Light bases.** The lightest colors (Taupe 1, Cirrus 1, Hedge 1, Violet 1, or their
  50–25% tints) make good bases. **White must still read as distinct** against the
  base.
- **The layering recipe:** 1 base, 1–2 color layers, 1 small top accent. Blur
  heavily, and avoid hard-edged color blocks. Examples from the manual:

| Base | Layer 1 | Layer 2 | Top accent | Feel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `#F7FBFD` Cirrus 50% | `#5DCBA2` Lagoon 2 | `#9CF0F2` Cirrus 3 | `#F6FFCD` Citrus 1 | Light, fresh |
| `#F1FEF7` Lagoon 25% | `#B09DE8` Violet 2 | `#9867FF` legacy Violet | `#F98D55` legacy Citrus | Light, warm pop |
| `#FFFAF7` Berry 50% | `#B5EF71` Hedge 2 | `#368792` Lagoon 3 | `#E5FF0C` (≈ Citrus 2) | Light, energetic |
| `#F9F9F8` Taupe 50% | `#C9E7F8` Cirrus bridge | `#14404F` legacy Lagoon | `#9867FF` legacy Violet | Neutral, deep |
| `#114753` Lagoon 4 | `#368792` Lagoon 3 | `#0A2028` Galaxy | `#B09DE8` Violet 2 | Dark, calm |
| `#0A2028` Galaxy | `#7F3BFF` Violet 3 | `#B5EF71` Hedge 2 | `#9CF0F2` Cirrus 3 | Dark, vivid |

Countless other combinations work. Bring new explorations to the brand team.

## Accessibility (p. 59)

The manual lists these pairs as **WCAG AAA** and approved where accessibility is
critical:

| Background | Text | Contrast (computed) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Galaxy `#0A2028` | White | 16.8 : 1 ✅ AAA |
| Taupe 1 `#F5F4F3` | Almost Black | 16.4 : 1 ✅ AAA |
| Cirrus 1 `#F1F8FC` | Almost Black | 16.8 : 1 ✅ AAA |
| Hedge 2 `#B5EF71` | Almost Black | 13.3 : 1 ✅ AAA |
| Lagoon 4 `#114753` | White | 10.2 : 1 ✅ AAA |
| Violet 3 `#7F3BFF` | White | **5.3 : 1 ⚠ AA only** (AAA needs 7 : 1 for normal text) |

White on Violet 3 meets AAA only for large text (18pt and up, or 14pt bold). Keep body
copy off Violet 3 when accessibility matters.

## Product UI colors (p. 60)

Buttons, links, confirmations, warnings, and errors inside the product follow the
**Quotient Design System**, not this palette. They're intentionally more neutral and
restrained. Don't use them in marketing. Contact the **Product Design** team.

## Open questions for Brand

1. **Duplicate labels.** On p. 50, Hedge's Dark swatch `#074F37` is labeled
   "Hedge 4" (the real Hedge 4 is `#338B4E`), and Citrus Dark `#C64444` is labeled
   "Citrus 4". The p. 49 spectrum labels both as "Dark", and this file follows p. 49.
2. **Cirrus tint labels.** P. 50 labels the Cirrus tints "Tint of Lagoon 1", but p. 49
   says "Tint of Cirrus 1". This file follows p. 49.
3. **RGB typos.** Lagoon 3 `#368792` lists RGB 182/240/113 (that's Hedge 2's). Citrus 1
   `#F6FFCD` lists 250/232/222. Hex is treated as authoritative here.
4. **Pantone typos.** Berry 1 and Taupe 1 share 663 C. Citrus 1 and Berry 2 share
   475 C. What are the correct values?
5. **Violet 3 contrast.** White on `#7F3BFF` is 5.3 : 1, which is AA, not AAA as
   labeled. Should the label change, or should body text be restricted on Violet 3?
6. **Near-palette values in examples.** The layering examples use four values that are
   each one step off a palette value: `#9967FF` (vs `#9867FF`), `#F5FFCC` (vs
   `#F6FFCD`), `#E5FF0C` (vs `#E6FF0C`), and `#F6D0BC` (vs `#F7D1BD`). Are these
   typos?
7. **Site colors.** captivateiq.com uses lavender `#E7C0FF`, which isn't anywhere in
   the manual. (The site's teal `#2D7B81` turns out to be a legacy Lagoon color, p. 49.)
8. **Corner radius.** The website's primary CTA uses a 14px radius, but the manual's
   rounding system is built on 4/8/16/24px (see
   [graphic-language.md](graphic-language.md#product-vignettes-pp-116122)). Which is
   the standard for marketing buttons?
