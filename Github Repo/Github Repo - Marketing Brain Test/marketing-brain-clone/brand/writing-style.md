# Writing style

The mechanics side of writing on brand. For how it should sound, see
[voice-and-tone.md](voice-and-tone.md).

## Structure

- **Write for scanners as well as readers.** Group related ideas, and use descriptive
  headers and subheaders.
- **Lead with the main point.** Put the most important content first and build a
  clear hierarchy beneath it.

## Sentences and words

- Use **active voice** and **positive language**.
- Use **short words and short sentences**.
- **Cut unnecessary modifiers.**
- Use **specific examples**. Avoid vague language.
- **Use contractions** more often than not ("we're", "it's", "don't").
- **Read it out loud** when in doubt, to check that it sounds natural.

## Punctuation and formatting

- **Use the Oxford comma.** "Plan, pay, and prove", not "plan, pay and prove".
- **Exclamation points:** one at most, and ideally none (see
  [voice-and-tone.md](voice-and-tone.md#avoid-p-24)).
- **Don't underline**, and don't combine italic, caps, and underline in any mix. For
  emphasis, use a heavier weight in body text or the IvyPresto italic accent in
  headlines (see [visual/typography.md](visual/typography.md)).
- **Be consistent.** Follow the patterns in this guide over personal preference.

## Not covered by the manual yet

The manual doesn't set rules for these. Until Brand decides, follow the existing
house practice and flag the gap.

- Headline case (sentence case or title case)
- Numbers and percentages (numerals or words, and "%" or "percent")
- Dates, times, and time zones
- Em dashes and spacing (the manual itself uses spaced em dashes, " — ")
- Abbreviations on first use (ICM, SPM, GTM)

For names and capitalization, see [word-list.md](word-list.md).
