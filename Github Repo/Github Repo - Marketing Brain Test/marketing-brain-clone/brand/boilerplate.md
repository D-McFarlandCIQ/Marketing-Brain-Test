# Approved boilerplate

Use these **verbatim** for press releases, event listings, speaker bios, partner
pages, and anywhere else the company needs a standard description. Don't paraphrase,
merge, or "improve" them. If none of the lengths fits, ask Brand. Don't write a new
one.

## 100 words

> CaptivateIQ is rebuilding compensation and sales planning for the age of AI. The
> platform's AI agents are built directly into SmartGrid, CaptivateIQ's real-time
> modeling architecture, designing, running, and adapting the systems behind
> go-to-market execution with the speed and precision today's revenue leaders demand.
> To learn why CaptivateIQ is the intelligent sales performance management (SPM)
> platform trusted by industry-leading organizations such as Affirm, Boston
> Scientific, and Netflix, visit www.captivateiq.com.

## 50 words

> CaptivateIQ is rebuilding compensation and sales planning for the age of AI. Its AI
> agents are built into SmartGrid, CaptivateIQ's real-time modeling architecture,
> designing and adapting go-to-market systems with the speed and precision revenue
> leaders demand. Learn why leading organizations like Affirm, Boston Scientific, and
> Netflix trust CaptivateIQ at www.captivateiq.com.

## 15 words

> CaptivateIQ rebuilds compensation and sales planning for the AI era with
> intelligent, AI-native SmartGrid technology.

## Notes

- The named customers (Affirm, Boston Scientific, Netflix) are approved **inside this
  boilerplate**. That doesn't make them approved proof points for other copy. Check
  `positioning/proof-points.md` once it exists.
- These descriptions are newer than the 2024 vision, mission, and promise lines in
  [foundations.md](foundations.md). When the two conflict, the boilerplate is the
  current language.
