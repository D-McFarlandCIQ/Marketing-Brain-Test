# Voice and tone

**Voice** is who we are, and it doesn't change. **Tone** is how we adjust that voice
to the reader and the moment. A social post and a support email to a customer who's
having a bad day share a voice but not a tone. Before you pick a tone, think about
what the reader is feeling.

## The voice

Always **conversational, natural, and friendly**, and always built on the five
personality traits from [foundations.md](foundations.md):

| Trait | In writing, that looks like |
| --- | --- |
| **Confident** | State things plainly. Back claims with specifics. Don't hedge what we know. |
| **Grounded** | No bragging. Make the customer's result the story, not us. |
| **Approachable** | Write as if any question is welcome. No condescension, no ego. |
| **Unorthodox** | Skip the stale enterprise-SaaS phrasing. A fresh angle is fine if it's earned. |
| **Empowering** | Write from the side of admins and payees. Show what they can now do. |

The right-hand column is MOps's reading of the trait definitions. The manual defines
the traits but doesn't give writing-specific guidance for each one.

## Good content comes off as (p. 24)

- **Human**, and not straight out of an LLM (the manual says this outright)
- **Relatable**
- **Well-intentioned**
- **To the point**
- **Clever, but never rude, judgmental, or arrogant**

## Avoid (p. 24)

- **Slang, jargon, or overly casual language.**
- **Exclamation points.** More than one is too many, and none is better.
- **Emoji in formal communication.** Social media is the exception.

## Dialing tone by situation

The manual says to adjust tone but doesn't give a channel-by-channel table. Until
Brand provides one, use the Brand Star Equalizer
([foundations.md](foundations.md#brand-star-equalizer-p-20)) and turn traits up or
down. The rows below are a MOps starting point for discussion. Brand hasn't approved
them.

| Situation | Turn up | Turn down |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Social, events, swag | Unorthodox, Empowering | — |
| Thought leadership, analyst-facing | Confident | Unorthodox |
| Customer support or an incident | Approachable, Grounded | Unorthodox |
| Sales and competitive content | Confident, Grounded | — |
| Product UI and in-app guidance | Approachable | Unorthodox |

## Notes for assistants

- If you need a company description, use [boilerplate.md](boilerplate.md). Don't
  write your own.
- Read the draft aloud, in effect. If a sentence sounds like generic AI marketing
  copy, rewrite it. The brand calls this out specifically.
