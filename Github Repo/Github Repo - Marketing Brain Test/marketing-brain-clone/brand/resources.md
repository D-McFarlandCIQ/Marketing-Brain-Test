# Brand resources

## Requesting brand design work (p. 204)

1. Open **Notion → General → Brand**, then the **"Brand Design Requests"** tile.
2. Fill in the short form. It covers both visual and verbal requests.
3. Corporate Marketing (Jarrod Ryhal or Lindsey Bly) will follow up.

**Timing:** urgent requests are reviewed **weekly**, and medium or low priority ones on
a **two-week cycle**, queued as they arrive. Give as much notice as you can, even if
the content isn't final. For large cross-functional campaign requests, go to
**Lindsey Bly** directly.
