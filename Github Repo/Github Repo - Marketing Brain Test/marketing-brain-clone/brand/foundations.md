# Brand foundations

Who CaptivateIQ is, what it stands for, and how its personality is meant to come
across. Everything in [voice-and-tone.md](voice-and-tone.md) and the visual files
builds on this page.

## Origin story (p. 7)

Co-founders Mark Schopmeyer and Conway Teng had managed commissions themselves and
felt the pain firsthand. Calculation errors cost revenue, and opaque payouts cost
payees' trust. They built a purpose-built ICM solution to incentivize the right
selling behavior without those costs.

The manual describes the payoff as a three-part transformation:

- Admins become strategic GTM partners.
- Payees become celebrated difference-makers.
- Companies deliver greater revenue performance.

## Personality: the Brand Star (pp. 12–20)

Five traits, each with supporting traits. Each trait sits in tension with two
others, which keeps any single trait from tipping into a caricature.

| Trait | Supporting traits | What it means | In tension with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Confident** | Informed, intentional, expert | We know our customers' needs and industry best practice, we make and keep promises deliberately, and we're the go-to authority in our space. | Unorthodox, Approachable |
| **Grounded** | Humble, forthright, admirable | Being the leader in SPM doesn't go to our heads. Customer success is what matters. | Empowering, Unorthodox |
| **Approachable** | Attentive, curious, collaborative | Customers and partners can bring us any question, big or small, and never get an oversized ego in return. | Confident, Empowering |
| **Unorthodox** | Flexible, inventive, maverick | We don't follow the status quo. We take thoughtful risks and make big bets we believe will pay off. | Confident, Grounded |
| **Empowering** | Passionate, dynamic, motivational | Our founders managed commissions themselves, and we're driven to help customers solve every sales performance challenge. | Grounded, Approachable |

### Brand Star Equalizer (p. 20)

Treat the five traits as dials and turn each one up or down to suit the moment. The
equalizer is also a way to judge a piece objectively: score each trait and ask
whether the mix fits the audience and goal.

The manual's example scores one piece at Unorthodox 4, Grounded 3, Empowering 2,
Confident 2, Approachable 1 (on a 4-step scale). That's an illustration, not a
default mix.

## Brand essence (p. 21)

**Wonder + Intelligence.** Everything should give customers a sense of both. The
color palette is described the same way (see [visual/color.md](visual/color.md)).
