# Word list

Names and terms as the brand manual spells them. This isn't yet a full
use / avoid / never-say list, because the manual doesn't include one. Add to it as
Brand and PMM decide.

## Company

| Use | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **CaptivateIQ** | One word, with a capital C, I, and Q. Use it on first reference and in formal copy. |
| **CIQ** | The accepted shorthand: "so many of our beloved customers and partners know us simply by" CIQ (p. 38). Fine after first reference and in internal or social copy. |

Don't use "Captivate IQ" (with a space), "CaptivateIq", or "Captivate".

## Platform and products

| Term | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **SmartGrid** | CaptivateIQ's real-time modeling architecture. The AI agents are built into it. One word, with a capital S and G. |
| **CIQ Incentives** | Product pillar: incentive compensation. |
| **CIQ Planning** | Product pillar: sales and compensation planning. |
| **CIQ Catalyst** | Product pillar. |
| **CIQ Agents** | The AI agents and AI offerings, spanning the other pillars. |
| **CIQ Eclipse** | Appears in the manual's in-product video examples (p. 200). Confirm with PMM before using it externally. |

Product lockups put the product name after the CIQ wordmark (see
[visual/logo.md](visual/logo.md#product-lockups-pp-6188)). In running text, use "CIQ
Incentives" or "CaptivateIQ Incentives". Confirm with PMM which one is preferred.

## Internal names (don't use in customer copy)

| Term | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Quotient** | The product design system: UI colors, components, Font Awesome icons. |
| **Calcy** | The "official / unofficial" mascot. Limited use at customer and internal events, and always as an accent, never as the primary brand identifier (p. 185). |
| **IQ Bug** | The smallest logo mark (see [visual/logo.md](visual/logo.md)). |
| **"Driving Force" / "Business Driver" angle** | The 20° graphic device (see [visual/graphic-language.md](visual/graphic-language.md)). |

## Category terms

| Term | Expansion | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SPM** | sales performance management | The boilerplate calls CaptivateIQ "the intelligent sales performance management (SPM) platform". Spell it out on first use. |
| **ICM** | incentive compensation management | Used in the origin story ("purpose-built ICM solution"). |
| **GTM** | go-to-market | Spell out "go-to-market" in external copy, as the boilerplate does. |
| **BPM** | — | Appears once (p. 145) beside ICM and SPM. Its meaning isn't defined, so confirm before using it. |

## General avoid list (from voice and tone)

- Slang, jargon, and overly casual language
- Exclamation points (one at most)
- Emoji outside social media
- Copy that reads as if "straight out of an LLM"
