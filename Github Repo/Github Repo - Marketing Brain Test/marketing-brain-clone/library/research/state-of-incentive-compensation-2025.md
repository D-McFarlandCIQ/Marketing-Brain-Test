## Method

- Online survey through commercial B2B panels. n=205, 13-minute median completion.
- U.S. respondents involved in incentive comp decisions, excluding C-level and junior managers.
- Companies with 500+ employees that sell to businesses and have a sales team. Excludes
  government, education, and non-profits. Size quotas: 500–1K (26%), 1K–5K (39%), 5K+ (35%).
- Repeats an earlier 2024 wave, so some stats have year-over-year comparisons.

## Key stats

**How comp is run**
- 70% use formal ICM software, 47% use spreadsheets, 31% use custom builds. 14% use only spreadsheets.
- Only 27% have automated commissions end to end.
- Reporting and analytics, commission calculations, and data integration are seen as the
  areas that would benefit most from automation.
- Teams spend a median 36 hours a month processing payouts, plus about 26 hours each on
  approvals and fixing errors.

**Planning cadence**
- 43% review incentive performance quarterly, but only 35% adjust plans quarterly. 45%
  adjust annually or ad hoc, which the report calls "a big gap between knowing and doing".

**Accuracy and confidence**
- 48% both overpaid and underpaid sellers in the past year. 29% say they never do.
- Only 25% are very confident their plans drive the behavior they want.
- 30% feel very prepared for market shifts; 55% only somewhat prepared.
- 93% get pay inquiries from reps, most often 11–20 per pay period.

**ROI and priorities**
- Top challenge: lack of visibility into comp program ROI. Difficulty getting exec
  buy-in to improve the system is ranked #1 most often (14%).
- Top objectives: supporting growth initiatives (59%), seller productivity (56%), seller motivation (53%).
- Barriers to upgrading: already evaluating (30%), budget (23%), unaware of current inefficiencies (15%).

**AI**
- 22% use AI extensively and 42% to some extent. Of AI users, 67% use AI built into their
  ICM software and 71% use general tools like ChatGPT.
- Non-users cite a lack of internal expertise (40%), budget (33%), and integration (33%).

## Associations worth using in narrative

These come from exploratory tree models. They are correlations, not causal claims.

- Companies that adjust plans weekly or monthly reported significant (10%+) revenue
  growth 46% of the time, vs 14% for quarterly or annual adjusters.
- Extensive AI users: 72% very prepared for volatility.
- Companies using ICM software: 36% very prepared, vs 18% without.

These support the V6 growth story: frequent, live plan changes go with growth.

## CaptivateIQ brand metrics (internal only)

- Unaided awareness 12% (8% in 2024). Aided awareness 43% (18% in 2024). Usage 11% (9% in 2024).
- Aided awareness of competitors: Salesforce Spiff 80%, SAP Callidus 49%, Xactly 33%,
  Performio 29%, Everstage 29%, QuotaPath 28%, Varicent 21%, Anaplan 19%, Forma.ai 16%.
- Satisfaction scores by brand have very small samples (CaptivateIQ about n=12). Don't cite them.
