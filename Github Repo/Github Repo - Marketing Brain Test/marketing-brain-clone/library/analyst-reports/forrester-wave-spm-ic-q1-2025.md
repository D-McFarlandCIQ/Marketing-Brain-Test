## What Forrester said about us (summary)

- **Placement:** second-highest strategy score and third-highest current offering among 12 vendors.
- **Strengths:** a wide range of preconfigured integrations makes data ingestion easy.
  SmartGrid's spreadsheet-based UI is easy for admins and gives fast time to value.
  Top marks on plan creation, modeling and scenario planning, data transformation,
  time to value, advanced AI, ease of implementation, innovation, roadmap, and pricing
  flexibility.
- **Weaknesses:** no mobile app; administration controls and compliance need to be more
  comprehensive; customers want gaps closed in core SPM/ICM features. Lowest marks on
  extended feature integrations, controls/compliance/administration, and access through
  other applications.
- **Best fit:** companies that want to empower administrators to manage their own comp needs.
- **Strategy note:** moving into territory and quota is expected, but it puts us in a
  crowded space where we must find innovative advantages to stand out as a broader SPM solution.

## Market themes Forrester calls out

- Systems integrators are no longer mandatory, so buyers should include SI costs when comparing vendors.
- Dedicated SPM customer success beats generalist CSMs at large vendors.
- GenAI, scenario planning, and CRM integration differentiate, but few reference
  customers have deployed them. Ease of use, complex calculations, and data
  transformation matter most today.
