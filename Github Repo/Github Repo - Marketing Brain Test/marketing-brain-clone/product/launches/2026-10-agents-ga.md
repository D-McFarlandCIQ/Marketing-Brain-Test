## What it is

See [capabilities/agents.md](../capabilities/agents.md). Headline: "The planning stage
of the growth cycle now works end to end: Rev Planning sets quotas and coverage on live
data, Comp Builder builds and changes the plans underneath in plain language, and
nothing moves without an admin approving it."

## Key messages

- Make the change your strategy calls for, without a consultant and without breaking what's underneath.
- You orchestrate. Agents execute.
- Nothing gets the GA label until it works on your data and is trustworthy.
- Built on your real data, fully governed, with humans always in the loop.
