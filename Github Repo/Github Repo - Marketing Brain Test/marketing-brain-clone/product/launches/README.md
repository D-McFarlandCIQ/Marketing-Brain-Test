## Tiering (Launch Framework 2.0, Oct 2024)

> The framework is two years old and points to a Notion page as the live version, and the
> directory asks whether a release process exists at all. Confirm with PMM (Vijay) before relying on it.

| Tier | Criteria | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tier 1** | Substantially improves value prop and competitive positioning. Significant change for most users. Completely new process or talk track for CX and/or Sales. Rolled into **quarterly product launches**. | PLM, NPX / what-if |
| **Tier 2** | Moderate change to value prop and positioning. CX, UI, or workflow change for most users. Some impact on the sales talk track. | Sandbox |
| **Tier 3** | Minor (if any) change to value prop. Small change for most users, or a big change for few. No change to the sales talk track. | Payout filters |

Projects under 2 weeks of engineering work, or Tier 3 GTM releases, skip the launch artifacts.

### What each tier gets (each tier includes the one below)

| Workstream | Tier 3 | Tier 2 adds | Tier 1 adds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Market awareness | Product tour, web and screenshot updates (PMM), as needed | PMM scopes with Marketing: customer quote, video, web, PR, blog, social, events, newsletter, ads | Same, at full scale |
| Sales enablement | SE technical enablement; sales release log | Ad hoc training, golden org, talk track and collateral updates | One-pager/data sheet, pricing and packaging SKU, certification, pitch deck updates |
| Customer enablement | Knowledge Center, newsletter, release notes, roadmap slide | In-app notification, training updates, micro-learning, demo video | Customer webinar |
| CX enablement | CX toolkit lite | CX toolkit medium, activity guide | CX toolkit heavy, guided training |
| Internal | #product-launches post | Internal demo video | Company all-hands, post-launch recap (PMM) |

### Launch types

- **Market announcement:** several launches told as one story (e.g. "Enterprise controls" = Sandbox + RBAC).
- **Product launch:** a separately paid offering (e.g. Sandbox, PLM).
- **Feature launch:** a single feature of any tier.

### Phases

1. Define problem (one-pager) → 2. Align on solution (PRD, tier sizing) → 3. Prepare
for launch (GTM checklist, CX enablement toolkit) → 4. Adoption (analytics dashboard).

## Gaps

Release calendar (Product), GTM calendar (PMM + Corporate Marketing), release notes and
